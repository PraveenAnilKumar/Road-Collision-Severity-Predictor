About the project

Safer roads start with better insight.

The Road Collision Severity Predictor uses collision details to estimate whether an incident may be fatal, serious, or slight. It is a data science project designed to explore road safety patterns and make collision information easier to understand.

Try the predictor

How it works

The project prepares road collision records and related vehicle information, then uses machine learning models to estimate collision severity from the details provided.

Predictions are estimates for learning and exploration. They should not be used as a substitute for official safety guidance or emergency services.
